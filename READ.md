# Pythonia POS System

A Python-based Point of Sale (POS) booking system developed for Pythonia Serviced Apartments as part of the RMIT Programming Fundamentals (COSC2531) assignment.

## Project Overview

This application is designed for booking managers to manage apartment reservations for guests staying at Pythonia Serviced Apartments.

The system supports:
- Apartment bookings
- Guest management
- Reward point calculation
- Supplementary item ordering
- Booking receipt generation
- Apartment and product management
- Guest booking history tracking

The company manages three apartment buildings:
- Swan
- Goose
- Duck

Guests can stay between 1–7 nights and optionally order supplementary services such as breakfast, car parking, toothpaste, and extra beds.
